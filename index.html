Featured ingredient

Centella asiatica

A small, unassuming leaf that has calmed skin for centuries — and one of only five actives we build most of our formulas around.

Also known as tiger grass, centella has been used in traditional wound care for generations before skin care brands caught on. We use a cold-extracted form that keeps its calming compounds intact, then let it do most of the talking in our cleansers and oils.

  • Calms visible redness — often within 48 hours of consistent use.
  • Supports the skin barrier — pairs well with actives like retinoids and vitamin C.
  • Gentle enough for daily use — including on reactive, post-procedure skin.
